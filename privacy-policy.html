Privacy Policy

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Tourly ("we", "our", or "us"). At Tourly, we value the trust you place in us when you share your personal details for holiday planning, hotel reservations, and sacred pilgrimage journeys across India.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, process, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website (tourly.com), book tour packages, or communicate with our travel advisors.

2. Information We Collect

To provide customized travel itineraries, yatra logistics, and hotel bookings, we collect the following types of information:

  • Contact Details: Full name, email address, phone number, and physical mailing address.
  • Travel & Identity Proofs: Government-issued ID numbers (Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID) required for security clearances, temple VIP passes, and train/flight ticket issuance.
  • Payment Information: Transaction identifiers, billing addresses, and payment confirmations processed securely through RBI-approved payment gateways. We do not store raw credit/debit card credentials on our servers.
  • Traveler Preferences: Dietary requirements, emergency contacts, medical notices for high-altitude yatras (e.g., Kedarnath/Amarnath), and room accommodation preferences.

3. Pilgrimage Permits & Yatra Verification Data

For sacred destinations such as Kedarnath, Ayodhya Ram Mandir, and Varanasi Ganga Aarti VIP boat bookings, local administrative trusts and government authorities mandate specific traveler registration.

Special Advisory for Sacred Yatras: Identification documents provided for helicopter passes, yatra e-permits, or biometric tokens are strictly shared only with authorized government departments and shrine boards (such as BKTC, Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra).

Such documentation is purged from our active processing pipelines within 30 days after the completion of your itinerary.

4. How We Use Your Information

We collect and process your information exclusively for legitimate travel operations:

  • Confirming travel bookings, airline seats, luxury coach transfers, and hotel vouchers.
  • Sending booking updates, live weather forecasts, road condition advisories, and emergency alerts.
  • Providing 24/7 on-ground customer support through phone and WhatsApp while on tour.
  • Delivering our newsletter with exclusive travel deals (only if explicitly opted-in).

5. Third-Party Sharing & Service Partners

Tourly never sells, rents, or monetizes your personal data. We only disclose necessary traveler information to trusted travel fulfillment partners:

  • Airlines & Rail Carriers: Passenger details required for ticket issuance under civil aviation and railway norms.
  • Partner Hotels & Ashrams: Guest details required for check-in verification and stay arrangement.
  • Payment Gateways: Encrypted financial communication with certified payment processors.

6. Data Security & Storage Protocols

We employ industry-leading technical and organizational safeguards to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse:

  • 256-bit TLS/SSL Encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is strictly encrypted.
  • Access Control: Only certified travel coordinators handling your specific booking have access to your details.
  • Encrypted Backups: Secure, redundant cloud storage protected by multi-factor authentication.

7. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Tourly uses lightweight cookies to improve your browsing experience, remember your currency preferences, and assess site traffic. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings at any time without impacting package browsing.

8. Your Privacy Rights & Choices

Under applicable Indian privacy guidelines and international data protection standards, you hold full authority over your personal information:

  • Right to Access: Request a full copy of the personal information stored in your account.
  • Right to Rectification: Update inaccurate contact details or travel party information.
  • Right to Deletion: Request removal of your data following the completion of your travel itinerary.

9. Inquiries & Data Protection Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact our Data Protection Team at privacy@tourly.com or reach out through our Contact Us page.
