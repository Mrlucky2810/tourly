Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book my holiday package?
We recommend booking international tour packages at least 4 to 8 weeks prior to departure to secure optimal flight tariffs and premier hotel suites.
What is your cancellation and refund policy?
We offer free cancellations up to 14 days before your departure date for 100% refund. Cancellations within 14 days are subject to hotel non-refundable deposits.
Do you assist with passport visas and travel insurance?
Yes! Our visa concierge team provides full documentation support, visa application guidance, and medical travel insurance options for all destinations.
Can I customize a package itinerary for a private group?
Absolutely. We specialize in custom group itineraries, private luxury transfers, and tailor-made sightseeing activities for families, weddings, and corporate groups.
