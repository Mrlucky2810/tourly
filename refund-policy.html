Refund & Cancellation Policy

1. Cancellation & Refund Policy Overview

At Tourly, we understand that unexpected personal emergencies, health issues, or family priorities can change your travel plans. We strive to provide a transparent, fair, and seamless refund process.

Because our itineraries involve advance reservations for private luxury vehicles, boutique hotel rooms, temple trust permits, and certified local guides, cancellation fees are tiered according to how far in advance notice is provided.

2. Standard Package Cancellation Timeline

The cancellation fee structure below applies to all standard Tourly holiday and pilgrimage tour packages, calculated from the time written cancellation notice is received:

Notice Period Before Departure Refund Eligibility Cancellation Fee Status
30 Days or More 90% of Total Tour Cost 10% Administrative Fee Maximum Refund
15 to 29 Days 75% of Total Tour Cost 25% Cancellation Fee Standard Refund
7 to 14 Days 50% of Total Tour Cost 50% Cancellation Fee Partial Refund
Less than 7 Days / No-Show 0% (Non-Refundable) 100% Tour Cost Non-Refundable

* Note: The percentage is calculated against the total cost of the package, regardless of whether a partial deposit or full balance was paid.

3. Sacred Yatra Exceptions & Non-Refundable Items

Certain components within customized and sacred yatra packages are governed by third-party statutory rules and cannot be refunded once issued:

  • Helicopter Darshan Tickets: Kedarnath, Char Dham, or Vaishno Devi helicopter passes issued via IRCTC/UCADA are 100% non-refundable once allocated in the traveler's name.
  • Temple VIP Passes & Special Poojas: Pre-booked VIP darshan tokens (e.g. Kashi Vishwanath Sugam Darshan, Ayodhya Aarti Passes) cannot be canceled or transferred under shrine board rules.
  • Air & Train Tickets: Commercial airline tickets and IRCTC train tickets follow the respective carrier's cancellation penalties plus standard agency handling charges.

4. Natural Calamities, Weather Disruptions & Force Majeure

Himalayan weather disruptions (cloudbursts, excessive snowfall, landslides) or elevated river water levels at the Varanasi ghats are unforeseen natural occurrences beyond human control:

Tourly Safe Travel Guarantee: In case a tour is stalled or canceled prior to departure due to certified government road closures or extreme weather advisories, travelers receive a 100% Tourly Credit Voucher valid for 12 months for any future travel package, with zero administrative penalty.

If an ongoing trip has to be truncated mid-route due to sudden roadblocks, unused hotel nights will be refunded subject to vendor release policies.

5. Refund Processing Time & Payment Method

Once your written cancellation is verified and approved:

  • Processing Window: Approved refunds are credited within 5 to 7 business days.
  • Payment Method: All refunds are automatically returned to the original mode of payment (UPI, Credit/Debit Card, or Net Banking) used during reservation.
  • Confirmation Receipt: A digital credit statement with banking reference ARN number is sent to your registered email immediately upon transfer.

6. Step-by-Step: How to Request a Cancellation

To ensure your notice timestamp is officially recorded, please follow these simple steps:

  • Step 1: Send an email from your registered email address to support@tourly.com with the subject: "Cancellation Request - [Your Booking ID]".
  • Step 2: State the reason for cancellation and traveler names included in the cancellation request.
  • Step 3: Our dedicated refunds team will review vendor releases within 24 hours and send you a formal confirmation breakdown.

7. Inquiries & Cancellation Assistance

If you need urgent assistance with your booking cancellation or date rescheduling, please contact our support desk at +91 98765 43210 or email support@tourly.com. You can also message us directly on WhatsApp.
