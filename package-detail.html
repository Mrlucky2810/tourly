Itinerary

5 Days plan • Meal plan: Breakfast & Dinner

Download Full Itinerary (PDF)

Arrive at Dubai International Airport (DXB). Meet your private chauffeur for transfer to your luxury 5-star hotel in Dubai Marina. In the evening, step aboard an illuminated traditional Marina Dhow Cruise for a 2-hour international buffet dinner, soft drinks, and live Tanoura folklore dance performances under the neon cityscape.

Embark on a guided morning city excursion covering the iconic Dubai Frame, Museum of the Future photo stop, and Jumeirah Beach. Afternoon visit to the world-famous Dubai Mall followed by fast-track VIP elevator access to the 124th & 125th observation deck of Burj Khalifa. Conclude with the synchronized Dubai Fountain evening show.

Enjoy a relaxed morning at the hotel pool. In the afternoon, a 4x4 Land Cruiser picks you up for an adrenaline-charged dune bashing experience across Lahbab's red dunes. Experience sandboarding, sunset photography, camel riding, henna tattoos, and a 5-star gourmet BBQ buffet dinner accompanied by fire shows and live Arabian music.

Full-day scenic drive to the UAE capital, Abu Dhabi. Tour the breathtaking white marble Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, drive past Emirates Palace and the Corniche waterfront, and stop for photo opportunities outside Louvre Abu Dhabi and Ferrari World on Yas Island.

Relish your final lavish breakfast at the hotel. Spend your free morning souvenir shopping for gold, spices, and perfumes in Old Dubai’s Deira Souks. Your private chauffeur will escort you to DXB International Airport in plenty of time for your scheduled departure flight.
