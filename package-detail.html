Package Overview

Escape to paradise on an all-inclusive 7-day tropical beach getaway. This curated journey combines luxury 5-star oceanfront resort accommodation with private speedboat island hopping, coral reef snorkeling excursions, and gourmet seafood dining.

Whether you are looking to unwind under palm trees, explore lush rainforest waterfalls, or cruise into the sunset on a private luxury catamaran, our experienced local guides will ensure every detail is handled seamlessly.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival & Resort Welcome Dinner

Arrive at the international airport where your private chauffeur meets you for a luxury escort to your 5-star oceanfront resort. Enjoy a welcome tropical cocktail and evening seafood buffet.

VIP Transfer Resort Check-In Welcome Buffet
Day 2

Island Hopping & Coral Reef Snorkeling

Board a private speedboat for a tour around 3 breathtaking tropical islands. Dive into turquoise waters to snorkel among vibrant coral reefs and exotic marine life with certified instructors.

Speedboat Tour Snorkeling Gear Beach Lunch
Day 3

Rainforest Trek & Jungle Waterfalls

Hike through ancient rainforest trails accompanied by an expert eco-guide. Take a refreshing swim in crystal natural pool basins beneath cascading jungle waterfalls.

Nature Hike Waterfall Swimming Eco-Guide
Day 4

Cultural Village & Artisan Cooking Class

Visit a traditional island village to experience local craftsmanship, spice markets, and take part in a hands-on culinary class preparing authentic regional dishes.

Cultural Tour Cooking Class Artisan Markets
Day 5

Sunset Catamaran Yacht Cruise

Spend a relaxed morning at leisure. In the late afternoon, set sail on a luxury catamaran yacht with live music, gourmet appetizers, and a sparkling beverage toast at sunset.

Catamaran Cruise Sunset Drinks Live Music
Day 6

Resort Wellness & Free Exploration Day

Indulge in a complimentary 60-minute full-body aromatherapy spa treatment at the resort wellness center. Enjoy free time for beach relaxation or boutique shopping.

Spa Massage Beach Leisure Shopping
Day 7

Souvenir Shopping & Airport Departure

Savor a final gourmet breakfast overlooking the sea before checking out. Enjoy souvenir shopping assistance followed by private transport back to the airport for your return flight.

Gourmet Breakfast Airport Transfer
