About The Destination

Varanasi, also known as Kashi or Banaras, is one of the world's oldest continuously inhabited cities and the spiritual capital of India. Located along the banks of the holy Ganges river, it is famous for its ancient stone ghats, grand evening Ganga Aarti rituals, and the sacred Shri Kashi Vishwanath Jyotirlinga Temple.

Pilgrims and travelers from across the globe gather here to experience sunrise wooden boat rides, chant prayers along the riverfront, visit historic temples, and savor authentic Banarasi street delicacies in timeless cobblestone alleyways.

Top Must-See Attractions

Dashashwamedh Ghat & Aarti

The most prominent ghat along the Ganges, renowned for grand evening brass lamp ceremonies.

Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir

Architectural marvel and holy birthplace shrine of Lord Ram in nearby Ayodhya.

Triveni Sangam Confluence

Sacred meeting point of holy rivers Ganga, Yamuna, and Saraswati in Prayagraj.

Suggested 4-Day Spiritual Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Kashi & Evening Ganga Aarti

Check into your boutique heritage hotel near the ghats. In the evening, attend the world-famous Ganga Aarti ceremony at Dashashwamedh Ghat with reserved boat seating.

Day 2

Sunrise Ganges Boat Ride & Kashi Vishwanath Darshan

Enjoy a serene 6:00 AM wooden boat cruise watching morning prayers on the ghats. Visit Shri Kashi Vishwanath Golden Temple and Annapurna Mandir for VIP darshan.

Day 3

Excursion to Sarnath & Dhamek Stupa

Drive to historic Sarnath where Lord Buddha delivered his first sermon. Tour the Dhamek Stupa, Ashoka Pillar, and Sarnath Archaeological Museum.

Day 4

Banarasi Silk Weaving & Local Food Tour

Explore traditional silk weaving workshops, taste famous Banarasi Tamatar Chaat and Kulhad Lassi, and capture final photographs before departure.
