4. Special Yatra & Temple Darshan Protocols

Tourly specializes in religious pilgrimages across sacred cities including Varanasi, Ayodhya, Prayagraj, Kedarnath, and Amritsar. Travelers must respect local sanctities:

Temple Guidelines & Dress Codes: Modest traditional attire is required at sanctified sites. Electronic devices, leather goods, and cameras are prohibited inside primary temple sanctums per shrine board regulations.
  • Darshan Timings: While Tourly arranges expedited or VIP passes whenever possible, darshan queues and aarti timings are governed entirely by local temple administration trusts.
  • Helicopter & Ropeway Tickets: Yatra helicopter shuttles (e.g. Phata/Guptkashi to Kedarnath) are subject to DGCA safety regulations, clear visibility, and biometric verification.
